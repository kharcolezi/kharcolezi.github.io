Research

Cementitious materials, experimental characterization, and functional materials

My research focuses on cementitious materials and their performance, from conventional and sustainable concrete to composites incorporating optical and functional elements. My work combines material design, laboratory testing, experimental characterization, and data analysis. Across my academic work, I have studied pervious concrete, low-carbon cementitious materials, optical fiber composites, mechanical performance, optical characterization, and functional material applications.

Doctoral research

Optical fiber-embedded cementitious composites

My doctoral research at ÉTS Montréal investigates cementitious composites containing optical fibers, with a focus on their optical, mechanical, and functional performance. The work examines how fiber configuration, material composition, and experimental conditions affect light transmission and mechanical behaviour, while also exploring applications such as visual privacy, image transmission, and interactive surfaces..

The project

A multidisciplinary doctoral research program

Over the course of my PhD, I developed an experimental research program combining cementitious material design, optical characterization, mechanical testing, microstructural analysis, image processing, and functional applications..

The project involved developing experimental methods and test setups, fabricating and testing specimens, analysing optical and mechanical data, and developing analytical and numerical approaches to interpret material behaviour.

Main research themes
01

Optical characterization

Experimental and theoretical investigation of light transmission in fiber-embedded cementitious composites, including the effects of fiber configuration and measurement conditions.

02

Mechanical performance

Mechanical behaviour of cementitious composites containing optical fibers, including fiber-matrix interaction, geometric effects, fiber orientation, and matrix composition.

03

Light transmission and visual privacy

Random fiber distributions for controlled light transmission, visual privacy, and digital image reconstruction.

04

Functional and interactive materials

Integration of optical fibers and sensing concepts into cementitious materials for interactive and responsive applications.

Approach

Experimental research from material design to interpretation

My work combines material preparation, experimental design, laboratory testing, instrumentation, and data analysis. Depending on the research question, I use mechanical testing, optical measurements, microscopy, image processing, analytical modelling, and numerical methods. An important part of my research is also methodological: identifying limitations in existing testing approaches, comparing results across studies, and developing clearer ways to characterize and report material performance. Results are communicated through peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, technical figures, and visual science communication.
